## Legend

- **Allowed use classes:**
  - `REUSE` — Open permissive licence (`CC BY`, `CC0`, `Public Domain`, `MIT`, `Apache-2.0`) with no ToS ban on automated/AI use: may adapt problems and structures directly with proper attribution. (Per LearningOS policy in `docs/sources.md`, LearningOS still prefers original, source-free problems via core cards unless attribution is explicitly preserved.)
  - `CORES` — Open copyleft/non-commercial/no-derivatives (`CC BY-NC`, `CC BY-SA`, `CC BY-NC-SA`, `CC BY-NC-ND`, `GFDL`, `GPL`) or all-rights-reserved materials that are publicly readable/downloadable without an anti-scraping/anti-AI ToS ban: **abstract mathematical cores and cognitive traps only** (`sources/_cores/<UNIT>.jsonl`); never copy, translate, paraphrase wording, or reuse numbers.
  - `NO-AUTOMATION` — Terms of Service or copyright policy explicitly forbids scraping, bots, automated data extraction, systematic harvesting, or AI/ML use (or materials are behind restricted institutional portals): **never scrape or automate**; only individual items the learner manually provides may be discussed or used as inspiration.
- **LearningOS topic codes:**
  - `MTH-T01` — Prealgebra & basic math (arithmetic, fractions/decimals, ratios/percentages, basic geometry/measurement, intro algebra/linear equations).
  - `MTH-T02` — Algebra, trigonometry & precalculus (sets, logic, real numbers, functions, polynomials/rationals, exponentials/logarithms, trigonometry, complex numbers, conic sections, sequences/series, vectors, matrices, counting/combinatorics).
  - `calculus` — Limits, continuity, differentiation, optimization, integration, area/volume, infinite series (planned post-`MTH-T02`; see `docs/backlog.md`).
  - `statistics` — Descriptive statistics, probability, random variables, normal/binomial distributions, inference, regression.
- **Fetch column:**
  - Direct URL — Open download page or repository with direct PDF/LaTeX/HTML files.
  - `manual` — Free public web portal where a human downloads selected PDFs/modules manually (no automated scraping).
  - `learner provides` — Restricted by ToS (`NO-AUTOMATION`) or paid/portal-gated; LearningOS never fetches automatically.
- **`(already have)`** — Source family already registered in `docs/sources.md`.
